Meghalaya

The Abode of Clouds

Basic Facts

① Statehood: 21st Jan 1972
② Capital: Shillong
③ Area: 22,429 sq. km.

Geography of Meghalaya

① Meghalaya is one of the seven sister states of northeast India.
② Consists mainly of Archean Rock.
③ The highest point in Megha is Shillong Peak, which is a prominent IAF station in the Khasi Hills, overlooking the city of Shillong. It has an altitude of 1961m.
④ The highest point in the Garo Hills is Nokrek Peak with an altitude of 1412m.

Climate

① Meghalaya has an average annual rainfall as high as 12,000 mm (470 inches).
② The Shillong area, with the highest elevations, experiences generally low temperatures.
③ The town of Sohra (Cherrapunjee) in the Khasi Hills is south of capital Shillong.
④ The village of Mawsynram, near Sohra, holds the record for maximum rain in a year.

Forest

The total forest area in Meghalaya is 8510 km² with only 993 km² under the control of the State Government.

The principal forest products include timber, bamboo, reed, broomstick, cane, ipecac, medicinal herbs and plants, cinnamon and thatch grass.

Living Root Bridges

Fascinating architectural wonders grown from the roots of ancient rubber trees by the Khasi and Jaintia tribes, found mainly in places like Nongriat.
